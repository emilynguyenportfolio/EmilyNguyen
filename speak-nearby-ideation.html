muse & customer

The Customer

Trinh T. Minh-ha is a Vietnamese-American filmmaker and writer whose ideology critiques binary thinking that obscures life's complexities, emphasizing that cultures are not monolithic but composed of diverse and often beautifully conflicting elements. She coined the idea of "speaking nearby" — as opposed to speaking for others — an approach that resists imposing meaning and instead makes space for multiplicity.

The Speak Nearby customer inhabits this same in-between: 18–30 year olds living in cities where cultures converge and meaning is constantly redefined — multi-hyphenate individuals, first and second generation immigrants, exchange students, and creative thinkers shaping new cultural languages of their own. Their style is a living collage.
