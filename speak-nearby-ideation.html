For the Speak Nearby collection, I developed a custom plaid unit in Photoshop — building a repeat tile from scratch by layering colored stripes, adjusting opacity and scale to mimic woven structure. The tile was then set into a seamless repeat block, ready for digital printing or screen application directly onto fabric.

Trinh T. Minh-ha is a Vietnamese-American filmmaker and writer whose ideology critiques binary thinking that obscures life's complexities, emphasizing that cultures are not monolithic but composed of diverse and often beautifully conflicting elements. She coined the idea of "speaking nearby" as opposed to speaking for others, an approach that resists imposing meaning and instead makes space for interpretation.

The Speak Nearby customer is a living collage of 18–30 year olds living in cities where cultures converge and meaning is constantly redefined.
