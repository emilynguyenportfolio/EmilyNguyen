Speak Nearby

~ translation is a fantasy

"Whereas content and language form a certain unity in the original, like a fruit and its skin, the language of the translation envelops its content like a royal robe with ample folds. For it signifies a more exalted language than its own and thus remains unsuited to its content, overpowering and alien. This disjunction prevents translation and at the same time makes it superfluous."
— Walter Benjamin, The Task of the Translator
Speak Nearby explores the untranslatability of cultural memory — the ways language fails to hold what the body already knows. The collection moves through sheer layers, worn materiality, and the quiet vocabulary of diaspora, asking what is lost and what quietly remains when we speak between tongues.
— Emily Nguyen, 2026
